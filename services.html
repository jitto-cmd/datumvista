Core Services

What we deliver

We handle large, complex studies across multiple geographies. Every service below has been delivered by our own team across real engagements — not subcontracted or described from textbooks.

01
Qualitative Research

Datumvista runs in-depth interviews, focus groups and ethnographic work, delivered face-to-face or remotely by experienced moderators. Studies are built to surface motivations and attitudes that surveys cannot reach, and are executed across the GCC and the other markets we operate in.

In-Depth Interviews (IDIs), Focus Groups, and ethnographic approaches that reveal motivations, attitudes, and emotional drivers beyond surface-level responses. Conducted face-to-face or remotely.
02
Quantitative Surveys

We collect survey data face-to-face (CAPI), by telephone (CATI), online and on paper, with structured field management and quality control behind every project. Large, complex sample sizes are handled across multiple locations in the GCC and beyond.

Face-to-face (CAPI), online, telemetric (CATI), and paper-based surveys. We handle large, complex sample sizes with structured field management and quality control across multiple locations.
03
Mystery Shopping

Mystery shopping is a discreet, visit-by-visit evaluation of customer experience and service quality carried out by trained field staff. Programmes cover retail, QSR, automotive, luxury beauty, telecoms and heavy machinery dealerships, and can run across Bahrain, the UAE, Kuwait, Oman and Saudi Arabia to a single protocol.

Discreet evaluations of customer experience and service quality across retail, QSR, automotive, luxury beauty, telecoms, heavy machinery dealerships, and other specialist environments.
04
Brand Tracking

Brand tracking measures awareness, perception, preference and competitive positioning over time, either continuously or in periodic waves. Each study shows how the brand is actually moving in the market between waves, so clients can act on the trend rather than a snapshot.

Continuous or periodic studies measuring brand awareness, perception, preference, and competitive positioning over time — helping clients understand how their brand is moving in the market.
05
Retail Distribution Mapping

Retail distribution mapping audits outlets across a market to record who stocks a product, where it sits and which competitors are present. Nationwide sweeps of cold stores, mini-markets and retail outlets expose missing stockists and distribution gaps that desk research cannot show.

Comprehensive nationwide outlet mapping — identifying missing stockists, auditing product placement, measuring competitor presence, and understanding distribution gaps across cold stores, mini-markets, and retail outlets.
06
HNI & Specialist Research

HNI research reaches individuals holding USD 5M+ in assets using established access and a methodology built around their time and discretion. It is used for premium financial, investment, real estate and lifestyle studies where standard consumer samples will not do.

Proven access and tailored methodology for interviewing High Net Worth Individuals with USD 5M+ in assets — a rare and carefully managed capability for premium financial, investment, real estate, and lifestyle research.
07
Telephone Mystery Evaluations

Telephone mystery evaluations test responsiveness, product knowledge and service standards over the phone across dealer networks and service centres. They can be run at multi-country scale without an in-person visit.

Structured telephone-based mystery audits across multi-country dealer networks and service centres — assessing responsiveness, product knowledge, and service quality standards without an in-person visit.
08
Price Surveys & Footfall Studies

Price surveys collect retail price data outlet by outlet, while footfall studies measure visitors to commercial and retail properties. Both feed competitive intelligence and leasing or investment decisions.

Retail price data collection across multiple outlets, and structured visitor measurement studies for commercial and retail properties — supporting competitive intelligence and investment decisions.
09
Online Surveys & Digital Research

Online and digital research is designed, hosted and deployed end to end, including panel management and data validation. Programmes run across banking, fintech, insurance and consumer product categories.

End-to-end design and deployment of digital surveys across banking, fintech, insurance, and consumer product categories — including panel management and data validation.
How We Work

Methodologies we master

The right methodology depends on the question. We advise on and execute across the full spectrum — often combining approaches within a single programme.

CAPI

Computer Assisted Personal Interviewing

Tablet-based face-to-face surveys with real-time data validation, GPS tracking, and quality control — replacing pen-and-paper with accuracy.

CATI

Computer Assisted Telephone Interviewing

Structured telephone surveys using trained callers with scripted questionnaires — ideal for large sample sizes and national coverage.

IDI

In-Depth Interview

One-to-one structured or semi-structured interviews lasting 30–90 minutes, conducted in-person or remotely, with expert moderators.

FGD

Focus Groups

Facilitated group discussions of 6–10 participants for exploratory research, concept testing, and consumer sentiment studies.

Online

Online Surveys & Panels

Self-administered digital surveys with panel recruitment, logic branching, quota management, and multi-device compatibility.

F2F

Pen & Paper Field Surveys

Traditional paper-based data collection for environments where digital tools are not appropriate — with rigorous manual QC processes.
